Our Story

  1. Inception

    MCC was built on hardware sales, finding early success in the market’s demands. Yet, this was only the beginning that set off a chain of events that reshaped the company’s vision, revealing a larger calling and a more profound purpose.

  2. Expansion

    Driven by unwavering dedication and hard work, MCC earned the trust of esteemed investors such as GOH Electronics and strategic industry partners including PNB, Duty Free, and Fujitsu. These alliances enabled the company to successfully evolve from hardware sales into a recognized authority in healthcare software innovation.

  3. Transformation

    The co-founding of Medix by Mr. Rosendo Go and Mr. Wilson Chan marked a defining moment for MCC. Built by Filipinos, for Filipinos, Medix is a revolutionary Hospital Information System. More than software, it was created with a singular purpose: to transform hospital systems and uplift Filipino lives.

  4. Impact

    Today, MCC remains at the forefront of healthcare innovation, dedicated to breaking down barriers and connecting every Filipino to the care they deserve.

From Profit to Purpose

What began as a search for a profitable venture grew into something far greater—a company with a calling to reshape the nation’s health. Today, Microcircuits (MCC) stands driven to strengthen Philippine healthcare. Uplifting lives today to ensure a better future for generations to come.

Healthcare Solutions

In partnership with the University of the Philippines Manila—one of the country’s foremost authorities in medicine—Microcircuits co-developed the Medix HIS and e-Med EHR systems to elevate healthcare service delivery and enable long-term financial sustainability for hospitals.

Medix Hospital Information System

Digitizes patient records to enhance hospital workflow efficiency, improve patient care quality, and secure medical data management.

Read more

This system is a comprehensive healthcare management solution designed to streamline hospital operations, including patient data management, billing and PHIC reimbursement, treatment coordination, and inventory and warehousing. It supports both web and Android tablet platforms, enhancing operational efficiency and public health security through integrated medical information management.

Medix E-claims Transmittal Portal

A streamlined web-based platform for submitting and tracking PhilHealth e-claims, reducing processing time, boosting PhilHealth reimbursements, and supporting hospital financial stability.

Read more

Specifically designed for healthcare providers, this system enables seamless PHIC claims submission and efficient management of electronic claims fully compliant with PhilHealth requirements. It streamlines the entire process, from claim creation and validation to eClaims transmittal and real-time status tracking, all within a single, secure, and user-friendly platform.

Medix Statistical Transmission Portal

Designed to support hospital regulatory reporting, this solution enhances data accuracy and facilitates the efficient, compliant submission of DOH Annual Hospital Statistics—reducing administrative burden and strengthening public health efforts.

Read more

This portal is a secure, standalone application designed to automate the collection, validation, and submission of annual healthcare facility statistical data to the Department of Health (DOH). The Medix Statistical Transmission Portal streamlines mandatory reporting on key metrics such as morbidity, mortality, bed occupancy, and service utilization—ensuring compliance, accuracy, and timely submission while significantly reducing manual workload.

Medix HCPN Referral & Navigation Portal

A centralized platform that streamlines patient referrals, tracks referral status, and efficiently connects patients to the appropriate care.

Read more

A secure, web-based platform integrated with the Medix Hospital Information System (HIS), enabling seamless electronic patient referrals and navigation across networked healthcare facilities in the Philippines. It ensures compliance with DOH and PhilHealth guidelines, reduces delays, and enhances continuity of care within integrated delivery systems.

eMed Primary Electronic Health Record

Prioritizes efficiency in primary care to improve patient outcomes through accessible, accurate, and secure health information management, while ensuring compliance with PhilHealth’s Konsulta Capitation Fund release requirements for LGUs.

Read more

A user-friendly, cloud-based platform designed to digitize and centralize patient health information for primary care providers, clinics, and small hospitals. It enables secure storage, easy retrieval, and seamless sharing of medical records—enhancing care coordination, reducing paperwork, and supporting compliance with healthcare regulations.
